# Market Comparison (research notes, October 2026)

## Australian competitors

| Provider | Offering | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate Scorecard (Equifax) | Financial Viability Assessments tiered by contract value, credit ratings, supplier monitoring | Main incumbent on government panels; report tiers range from express checks (< $0.5m) to comprehensive credit rating reports (> $10m) |
| Experian | Financial Viability Assessment | Three years of statements plus credit data; chartered-accountant prepared; 10-step risk rating tied to contract value and tenure |
| CreditorWatch | Financial Risk Assessment | Analyst review of 2–3 years of statements; also a CreditSource partner/channel |
| Big 4 / restructuring advisers (e.g. PwC) | Bespoke financial viability reviews | High-value or distressed situations |
| illion, Dun & Bradstreet | Credit reports and monitoring | Mostly automated, not analyst-written |

**CreditSource differentiators:** 48-hour turnaround, plain-English reports, free analyst
consultations, "no financials, no fee", contract-value-specific capacity analysis.

## vs Archer Third Party Risk Management (formerly RSA Archer)

| | Archer TPRM | CreditSource |
|---|---|---|
| Type | GRC software platform | Analyst-led service + data |
| Method | Questionnaires to third parties about internal controls | Collects actual financial statements |
| Scope | Broad: compliance, financial, infosec, reputation, resiliency, strategic, sustainability, fourth-party | Deep on financial viability (plus sanctions/PEP/adverse media monitoring) |
| Workflow | Exceptions, remediation plans, owners | Analyst narrative, mitigation advice, consultation |
| Who does the work | Your team | CreditSource analysts |

**Fit:** complementary. Archer as system of record; CreditSource ratings fed via API/CSV into
Archer's financial risk category instead of self-reported answers.

## vs individual credit checks (Experian, Veda/Equifax)

| | Individual credit check | CreditSource assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | A person (sole trader, director, guarantor) | A company's financial statements |
| Contents | Enquiries, accounts, repayment history, defaults, judgments, bankruptcy | Balance sheet, P&L, cash flow, 3-year trends, ratios |
| Orientation | Historical (lagging) | Forward-looking |
| Process | Instant, automated score | Analyst-reviewed, contract-specific |
| Regulation | Privacy Act credit reporting rules; permitted purpose/consent | Commercial data, supplier-provided |

**Fit:** pair them for small suppliers/sole traders — director check plus financial assessment.
