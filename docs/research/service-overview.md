# Service Overview (research notes)

Summarised from CreditSource's public website and capability statement (paraphrased).

## Services

| Service | Summary |
|---|---|
| Financial risk assessments | Forward-looking supplier viability assessed against the specific contract value; analyst-reviewed; 48-hour turnaround on standard reports once financials arrive (complex reports up to ~12 days) |
| Supplier financial monitoring | Ongoing, analyst-vetted alerts: liquidity, court actions, defaults, insolvency, payment delays, adverse media, PEP/sanctions, modern slavery reporting; traffic-light dashboard |
| Custom financial data | Hundreds of verified data points per company, with history, for ~50,000 AU/NZ companies a year; delivered via reports, dashboards, CSV or API |

Supporting features: plain-English reports, industry benchmarking, free post-assessment analyst
consultations, "no financials, no fee", pay-as-you-go or subscription pricing.

## Ordering process

Buyer supplies company name, ABN/ACN, contact details and contract value via the online portal.
If CreditSource does not already hold current financials, it contacts the supplier directly.

## The supplier's (vendor's) role

- Provides financial statements directly to CreditSource (buyer does not chase).
- Turnaround clock starts when the supplier's financials arrive.
- If no financials are provided, the buyer is not charged.
- May be monitored post-award through to warranty close-out.
- Is benchmarked against industry peers.
- No supplier-facing features (self-view, disputes, reusable profile) are described publicly.

## Financial statement structure

Sample reports show **three years side by side**.

**Balance sheet**
- Total current assets
- Total non-current assets
- Total assets
- Total current liabilities
- Total non-current liabilities
- Total liabilities
- Share capital
- Total equity

**Profit and loss** — net profit before tax (NPBT) and normalised EBITDA.

**Key ratios** — grouped by profitability, liquidity and debt levels (e.g. debt-to-equity,
gearing, cash as a share of assets, lease liabilities, related-party loans).

**Deeper assessments** (high-risk/high-value) — forecasts, work-in-progress schedules, forensic
review.

**Outputs** — risk score with probability of default (sample: 723 / 0.15%), industry benchmarks,
analyst narrative and mitigation recommendations.

The full data-product field schema is not public.
